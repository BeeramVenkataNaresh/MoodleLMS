This curriculum policy replaces the expectations outlined in The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 9 and 10: Canadian and World Studies, 2018. All Canadian and world studies programs for Grades 9 and 10 will be based on the expectations in the Grade 9 Exploring Canadian Geography (2024), Grade 10 History (starting in the 2026–27 school year) and Civics and Citizenship (2022) courses. The expectations for Grades 11 and 12 will continue to be based on The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 11 and 12: Canadian and World Studies, 2015, but all references to Grade 9 and 10 courses appearing in that document have been superseded.
